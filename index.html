Frequently Asked Questions ❓

Tap a question - all answers are here. Still stuck? WhatsApp us.

1. What is FIRSTSTORE-SA?

A small local shop in Devland, Soweto. We help you get fast cash by pawning your phone, laptop, TV, fridge or other items. We also sell tested second-hand items and help you sell a pawn before it expires.

2. How fast do I get paid?

Register in 10 seconds, verify with 3 photos, send item photo. We reply on WhatsApp in 5-10 minutes with an offer. If you accept, bring the item or arrange delivery and get paid.

3. What do I need to pawn?

1. SA ID or Passport. 2. Selfie holding your ID. 3. Proof of address. 4. The item must be yours, working, and not stolen. 5. Item photo.

4. How much can I get?

Up to R10,000 depending on item, condition, model and market value. Final offer is given after we see the item in person.

5. How long do I have to pay back / collect?

Your expiry date is on your pawn ticket / WhatsApp confirmation. You MUST collect before expiry. If you think you will miss it, use Auction My Pawn to sell before it expires.

6. What if my pawn expires?

If it expires and you did not collect or auction it, you lose the item. We cannot hold it after expiry. That is why we show a LIVE countdown on auctions.

7. What is Auction My Pawn?

If your pawn is about to expire, you can sell it while it is still pawned. A buyer buys your debt - they pay the release amount, you keep 20% profit. Example: Release R1,000 → Sell R1,200 → You keep R200.

8. Can I auction a pawn from ANOTHER shop?

Yes. Tell us which shop holds it, upload your pawn ticket, release amount and expiry date + time. Same 20% rule applies.

9. Are items for sale guaranteed?

Yes, we test every item. If an item has a fault we tell you before you buy. Ask on WhatsApp for photos, battery, and guarantee for that specific item.

10. Do you deliver?

Yes, in Devland / Soweto area. Delivery fee depends on distance. Or collect at 129, 3137 Hydra Street, Devland, 1811.

11. Is my ID safe?

We only use your ID to verify you are the owner and to protect you from fraud. We do not share it with strangers. See Privacy Policy.

12. Are you a licensed pawn shop?

We are a small, growing local business currently working on acquiring the correct operating paperwork and licences. We operate honestly as a small buy-and-sell / pawn service while we complete that process. See Terms for full disclosure.

Our online estimate is NOT a final offer. Final offer depends on brand, model, condition, age, accessories, market value, and whether it powers on and works. We may change or withdraw an offer after inspection.

Any fees, interest, storage or release amount will be explained on WhatsApp BEFORE you agree. If you do not understand, ask us. Do not agree until you are clear. We will not add hidden fees after you agree.

About Us 🏪

The real story behind FIRSTSTORE-SA.

Hi, I'm the owner of FIRSTSTORE-SA.
I'm a 34-year-old guy from Devland, Gauteng, Johannesburg. I'm a gamer. I love gadgets - always have.

I saw the gap: gadgets cost so much, and ordinary people just can't afford them. So I started doing what I love - buying from those who are selling, and selling at a very cheap price to those who need gadgets.

Why pawn? Because I know the pain.
I have lost a lot of things in pawn myself. I understand it. People pawn with good intention - they think they are going to get the money, buy the item back, and everything will be fine. But then they don't get the money in time, and they lose the item. Just like that. Gone.

After losing so much, I wanted to help people not go through the same thing. That's why I introduced Auction Your Pawn - so if you are about to lose something of good value, because another pawn shop gave you an unreasonable amount, you can auction it and get something better for yourself. You don't have to lose everything. You can retain something, or benefit from the item you are about to lose.

What I do every day:
✅ I search the internet, Facebook Marketplace and other places for items I can resell
✅ I build computers from scratch
✅ I refurbish furniture and appliances, then resell them
✅ I buy phones, laptops, TVs, fridges - test them, fix them, and sell them cheap

Why I started this:
I have tried a lot of businesses. I only did this one because I genuinely want to help people who are struggling, who are stranded financially during the month, to get access to cash by letting me hold some of their things - and then buy it back when they have room to breathe.

My promise:
✅ Fair price, always
✅ Paid fast
✅ Honest about paperwork - we are working to get fully licensed
✅ Your ID is protected
✅ If I can't help, I will tell you why
